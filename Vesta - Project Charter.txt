Project Description

Vesta is an AI-powered digital wardrobe application designed to help users organize their clothing and create suitable outfit combinations.

Problem Statement

Users often have many clothes but struggle to decide what to wear. They may also have difficulty matching colors, styles, occasions, and weather conditions.

Project Goal

The goal of Vesta is to provide personalized outfit recommendations using clothing that users already own.

Target Users

University students
Young adults
People interested in fashion
Users who want help choosing daily outfits

MVP Features

1. Digital Closet
2. AI Outfit Recommendation
3. Occasion and Style Selection
4. Weather-Based Outfit
5. Save Favorite Outfits
6. Outfit History

Success Criteria

Vesta should allow users to upload and organize their clothing, generate suitable outfit recommendations, save favorite outfits, and review their outfit history.
